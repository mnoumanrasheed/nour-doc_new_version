Final positioning I would use across the site 
The most important change is that NourDoc should no longer visually or verbally present itself 
primarily as an “AI scribe.” 
The website should establish this hierarchy: 
LEVEL 1 — CLINICIAN 
AI Clinical Documentation Assistant 
Android → Free Trial → Starter → Professional 
↓ 
LEVEL 2 — HEALTHCARE ORGANIZATION 
Ambient Clinical Intelligence Platform 
Cloud → Enterprise → On-Premises → EHR/EMR/HIMS 
↓ 
LEVEL 3 — HEALTHCARE OPERATIONS 
Enterprise Clinical Information Processing Platform 
Batch Processing → 1M+ encounters/day architecture → ICD-10 + CPT → Multiple Formats → 
Coding/Billing → Claims/Clearance → Integration 
That gives NourDoc a much stronger enterprise narrative without abandoning the simple consumer 
entry point. 
The single most important enterprise message 
I would make this visually prominent on the Medical Coding & Billing page: 
From Voice & Notes to Claim-Ready Documentation 
Process at enterprise scale. Structure clinical information. Support ICD-10 & CPT workflows. 
Deliver outputs in the formats your coding, billing and healthcare systems already use. 
And immediately below it: 
Designed to entertain 1+ million clinical encounters per day in appropriately configured enterprise 
environments. 
This wording is important: it presents 1M+ encounters/day as an architectural/platform capability, 
rather than implying that NourDoc currently processes that volume in production. 
Recommended global CTA hierarchy 
Primary: Try NourDoc Free 
Secondary: Book a Demo 
Enterprise: Talk to Enterprise 
Technical: Explore Integration 
This keeps the website simple for a physician visiting from the Android app while giving a hospital, 
coding company or enterprise buyer a clear path into the much larger NourDoc platform proposition.
